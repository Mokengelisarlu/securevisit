SecureVisit
Overview

SecureVisit is a multi-tenant Visitor Management SaaS platform built for organizations that need secure, scalable, and modern visitor check-in workflows.

The platform enables companies to manage visitors, hosts, departments, vehicles, security workflows, kiosks, and check-in/check-out operations through isolated tenant workspaces powered by subdomain routing.
